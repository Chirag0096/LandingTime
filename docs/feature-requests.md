# Landing Time — Future Feature Requests & Roadmap

## 1. Smart Probe Integration (Bluetooth Low Energy / Matter)
- **Concept**: Integrate directly with wireless meat probes (e.g., Meater, Combustion Inc, Typhur) via Matter protocol.
- **Benefit**: Eliminate manual delay reporting. When internal probe temperature velocity falls behind the predicted thermal curve, Landing Time automatically calculates a proposed replan and prompts: *"The chicken is heating 15% slower than expected. Would you like me to shift the asparagus by 7 minutes?"*

## 2. Multi-Zone Smart Oven Telemetry
- **Concept**: Integrate with smart ovens (Breville Joule, Anova Precision Oven, GE Profile) to read real-time compartment temperature and convection fan speeds.
- **Benefit**: Dynamic preheat compensation and multi-zone rack temperature management.

## 3. Alexa Shopping List Bidirectional Sync
- **Concept**: When an ingredient substitution occurs, automatically push the missing ingredient directly into the user's primary Alexa Shopping List or Amazon Fresh cart once Amazon exposes the write API through MCP.

## 4. Multi-Cook Workload Distribution
- **Concept**: Assign tasks to different family members or cooks based on skill level (e.g., "Cook 1: prep potatoes, Cook 2: sear asparagus").
